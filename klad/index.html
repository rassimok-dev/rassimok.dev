Клад

Collect every chest, then reach the exit — it stays shut until the last one is gone. Shoot a brick to open a way through it; it grows back. Guardians cannot be killed: shots pass straight through them, so this is routing and timing, not combat. Water and fire kill on touch. There is no jump.

Controls

← → ↑ ↓ or W A S D
Move and climb ladders
Space or Z
Shoot along the row you face — and start the game
P
Pause
R
Restart from the first level

Click the screen or tab to it to give it the keyboard. Losing focus pauses the game.
